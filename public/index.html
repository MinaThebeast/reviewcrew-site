FAQ

Straight answers.

Still unsure? The audit is free and there's no hard sell.

Do you fake reviews or buy 5-stars?

No. Never. We never create, buy or fabricate Google reviews, and we never gate requests to hide unhappy customers. We help you ask real customers after real jobs, and we draft authentic replies.

Who is ReviewCrew for?

Home-service businesses that win work from Google Maps and Local Services Ads: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, garage doors, pest control, cleaning and similar trades with a Google Business Profile.

What's in the free Review Gap Audit?

A scorecard comparing your rating, review count, reply rate and profile freshness against three local competitors. It comes with a plain-English read on what the gap is costing you and a clear recommendation.

Do I have to approve every reply?

You set the rules. Positive, low-risk replies can post under your style guide. Negative or sensitive reviews always come to you before anything goes live.

Why email first for review requests?

Email works from day one. Texting customers requires carrier (A2P) registration; once your number is registered we can add text timing to the playbook. Always permission-aware, never spam.

How fast can we start?

Your audit comes back in a few days. After signup, the desk is typically live within a week: style guide, request kit, reply workflow and your first report.

What's the setup fee for?

The $297 one-time fee covers profile intake, your competitor baseline, reply style guide, request templates and desk configuration.
