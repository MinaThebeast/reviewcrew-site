Privacy notice

Last updated September 26, 2026

What we collect

When you request a Review Gap Audit, we collect the details you type into the form: your name, business name, trade, city, Google Business Profile link, email address and phone number.

How we use it

  • To prepare your audit by reviewing your public Google Business Profile and nearby competitors' public profiles.
  • To contact you about your audit and, if you're interested, our services.

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it to post, write or request reviews on your behalf without your agreement.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers we use to run this website and deliver your request (for example, hosting and email delivery). They may process it only to provide those services to us.

How long we keep it

We keep audit requests only as long as needed to follow up with you and run our business. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or tell us to stop contacting you. Reply to any email from us to make a request.
